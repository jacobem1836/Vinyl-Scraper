Privacy notice

This is the whole of it. If something here is not clear, ask and we will fix the wording.

Who we are

CRATE is operated by Marriott Systems, Australia. For anything to do with your data — access, correction, deletion, or a question about this notice — contact [CONTACT EMAIL].

What we collect

  • Your email address, so you can sign in and so we can send the emails you asked for.
  • Your password, stored only as a bcrypt hash. We never hold the password itself and cannot read it.
  • The wishlist items and notes you enter, plus the listings we find for them.
  • If you joined the waitlist, the email address you gave us for that.

Why we collect it

  • To run your account and keep your crate separate from everyone else's.
  • To search stores and marketplaces on your behalf for the records you have listed.
  • To send emails you asked for: address confirmation, password resets, and deal alerts.

We do not sell your data, share it for advertising, or use it to build a profile of you.

Cookies

Exactly one cookie: a strictly necessary signed session cookie named crate_session, which keeps you signed in. There are no analytics cookies and no tracking cookies, which is why you are not being shown a consent banner.

Third parties

  • Resend delivers our email. To send you a message they receive your email address and the contents of that message.
  • Stores and marketplaces (Discogs, eBay AU, and the record shops we search) receive only the search text you entered. They are never told who you are, and your identity is not attached to those requests.

How long we keep it

Your account data is kept until you delete your account from the {% if user is defined and user %}Account page{% else %}Account page{% endif %}, which immediately removes your wishlist items and the listings attached to them. Waitlist email addresses are deleted on request.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of the data we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Email [CONTACT EMAIL] and we will action it. Deleting your account yourself does the same thing, faster.

Changes

If this notice changes in a way that affects what we collect or who receives it, we will update the date at the top of this page and tell you by email before the change takes effect.
