How it works

Three steps, then leave it alone.

  1. 1

    Add the records you want

    An album, an artist, a label, or a key word. Album searches pull suggestions straight from Discogs so you can pin the exact pressing rather than a near-miss.

  2. 2

    CRATE searches for you

    Discogs marketplace, eBay AU and 26+ Australian record stores, rescanned on a schedule. Irrelevant matches are filtered out before they reach you.

  3. 3

    See the real landed cost

    Every listing is converted to AUD with estimated shipping to Australia added, so a cheap US copy and a local one are finally comparable. Email alerts when a price drops.
